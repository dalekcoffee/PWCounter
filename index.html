Q & A

From Bushiroad's own documents, as of November 2026. Event staff always have the final word.

Can I use this app in a casual game?
  • Life, Material, Ingredient: allowed. They are numbers each player has and both players may check at any time; the rules require no particular tool. Keep the phone where your opponent can see it.
  • Souls as numbers: allowed when both players agree. You track how many souls you have and how many are standing.

Comprehensive Rules 3.2, 3.3 and 13.2

Can I use it at a Masters League event?
  • Life, Material, Ingredient: allowed with the phone in airplane / offline mode only. The app works fully offline.
  • Souls as numbers: not allowed. Players must bring a sleeved Soul Deck, and nothing allows tracking souls as numbers.
  • Play with a friend (online): not allowed. It needs a connection.

Masters League 26/27: Required Items, Third-Party Counters

What about other official or store events?
  • Life, Material, Ingredient: ask the organiser or head judge. The Floor Rules list life counters and Material and Ingredient markers as required tools (dice or beads may stand in), and say aid items with phone functions able to communicate generally cannot be used at official events, also recommended for sanctioned events.
  • Souls as numbers: only where the event's regulations allow it.

Floor Rules 2.3.5 and 2.3.10; Comprehensive Rules 13.2.1.1
