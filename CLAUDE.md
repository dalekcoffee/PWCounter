# PW Counter: build notes

Static PWA served by GitHub Pages at pwcounter.dalek.coffee. Vanilla ES modules, no build step, no npm dependencies, no backend, no login: every browser keeps its own game in localStorage (`pwc.state`).

- `js/state.js`, `js/calc.js`, `js/random.js` are pure and tested (`npm test`, node:test). `js/app.js` is the DOM.
- Style follows PalVault (dark palette, Figtree / Newsreader / JetBrains Mono). Day and night must stay obviously different on the board.
- Counter tiles: the whole left half is the minus button, the whole right half the plus button, with a visible divider and −/+ chips in ONE uniform colour (never a colour per sign). Counters never go below 0.
- Souls (`state.souls`, a normal setting since 0.2.7; style default `numbersCards`, the style options show only while souls are on): rules in state.js follow the official Comprehensive Rules (cited in its header); two players get Numbers or Numbers on cards (the card-only styles fall back to Numbers on cards), the card-only styles need one player.
- Tiles: the number sits on the tile's centre line, level with the −/+ buttons; icon and word above it. Never hide the Material / Ingredient words.
- Who goes first, two players (0.3.0; asked with or without souls since 0.3.5, `needsFirst`; one player only with souls; a room settles it by role): `#ov-first2` (sticky: a tap beside its buttons does not close it), pure rules in js/first-pick.js (Player 1 = seat 0 bottom, Player 2 = seat 1 top; die high 4–6 / low 1–3 or coin; one call, the other side gets the other; the call that comes up goes first). One player uses `#ov-first2` too (0.3.4: only the bottom side, `.f2-head`, a second big pick `.f2-big.solo`, the die / coin buttons shown in the pick step, `soloResultLine`; when the opponent wins you tap what they chose); the `#ov-first` sheet is only for rooms. `chooseFirst` starts a fresh game (0.3.12: `resetGame` + first; souls dealt after); a two-player no-souls game saved under way without `first` (pre-0.3.5) gets `first` = active in `cleanState` (`legacyFirst`) instead of being asked. `startGame` -> `announceBegin` (the `#begin` callout, one `.bg-side` per player saying Begin + You go first / second, 4 s; the turn button `.wait` for 10 s). Two players: `state.turnAt` (stamped in `commit` whenever the player or turn changes) drives `#turn-clock`. No landscape with two players (`row-land` hidden, `data-land` forced off).
- Life (0.3.8, pure in js/life.js): `lifeTone` colours the life number (board colour at 10, red toward 0, green toward 20 and above; it mirrors --num for day / night); `justLost` in `commit` shows `#over` (a second `.begin` callout, "Game over" + You win / You lose per side, 5 s) when a life reaches 0; 0.3.9: `fillFx` puts confetti (win) / rain (lose) in each side's `.fx`, transform-only, emptied after 5 s, skipped with reduced motion.
- Face to face (`stage[data-face=top]`, two players, flip on, the top player's turn): `.bar` and every `.overlay` are rotated 180° to face the active player.
- Two players look the same from both sides: sun / moon and sky mirrored about the middle, top seat labelled Player 2; `data-face=top` turns only `#ov-calc`, `#ov-dice` (settings stay with Player 1); `#ov-throw` is shared: `.throw-mirror` gives the top player the numbers, total and buttons, and two players wait `THROW_COOL_MS` (3 s) before Done / Roll again (`lockThrow`). Two-player Numbers on cards draws small cards (two rows of five bunched in the middle; rested = face down). Who goes first (0.3.4): the toss winner chooses Go first / Go second (`.f2-choose`, `firstSeat`); 0.3.13: the toss itself plays in `#ov-throw` (`throwForFirst`: one die via `rollNow(value, words)` / `flipNow(value, words)`, `#ov-throw[data-first]` hides the second die and the buttons, sticky; `firstThrowDone` returns to `#ov-first2` after `FIRST_SHOW_MS`).
- Counter taps (0.3.11): counted on `pointerdown` only; the `click` handler is for the keyboard (detail 0) and ignores a click within 1 s of a pointer press (`pressAt`), because older Android browsers send tap clicks with detail 0 (one tap counted twice). A second `pointerdown` on the same button within 40 ms is the same tap.
- Overlays are divs inside `#stage` (never a native dialog element, which would not turn with the landscape setting).
- Every file the app needs must be in `sw.js` SHELL (tested). The service worker caches one version whole (fetched with `?v=VERSION` past the CDN) and refuses a set where index.html (`meta pwc-version`) or js/version.js is from another version; files are never refreshed one by one (a stale index.html with new scripts killed every listener after the first missing element). Version lives in js/version.js, package.json, sw.js VERSION, index.html `meta pwc-version` + `css/app.css?v=`, app.css `--pwc-v` and CHANGELOG.md (tested equal); bump on every change. index.html's inline check (0.3.6) compares page, `--pwc-v` and `html[data-js]` (set by app.js) and, if they differ, unregisters the service worker, drops the pwc- caches and reloads once per version (sessionStorage `pwc.heal.<v>`).
- Performance (0.2.8, for old tablets): write the DOM through js/dom-write.js (`setText`, `setHidden`, `setData`, `setAttr`, `setProp` only write a changed value); save through `saveSoon()` (flushed on hide / pagehide); no `backdrop-filter` and no animated `filter` / `box-shadow` (animate opacity or transform on a layer instead); nothing animates by day; the dice shader stays loop-free; no colour transitions on tiles; every module is `modulepreload`ed in index.html (except the lazily imported js/qr.js + vendor, still in SHELL).
- Original design only: never copy another site's markup, art or branding.

## Online rooms (experimental, 0.1.0)
- Pure rules: `js/room-proto.js` (codes, messages, turn passing), `js/room-game.js` (mapping shared state / my seat), `js/drawer.js`, `js/qr.js`; `js/room-memory.js` + `js/room-sync.js` (remembered room, rejoin compare), `js/dice-anim.js`; connection `js/room.js` (transport factory, backoff); DOM `js/room-ui.js`. Relay in `worker/room/` stores nothing; one Durable Object per code, roles host/guest (`?role=&sid=`), a second host gets 4002 (the client draws a new code, max 5), a second guest 4001, the same `sid` replaces its own stale socket.
- The last room is remembered in localStorage `pwc.room` (code, role, phone sid, peer name, last peer seat + shared game; under 48 h; never other opponent data). Rejoin prompt on open; after a rejoin both phones exchange a `check` and, if anything differs, #ov-sync makes the host-arbitrated `pick`; shared messages are held until then.
- Each phone owns its seat (seat 0 = me); the opponent is held in memory only, never in localStorage. Shared: night, active (0 me / 1 them), turn, last writer wins by rev, host wins ties.
- Room mode forces the one-player layout (`stage[data-room=on]`); the opponent drawer is a div in `#stage` (stage coordinates, so landscape works). `?fakeRoom=1` on localhost uses a BroadcastChannel transport for tests.

## Watching and display mode (0.2.0)
- Relay role `watch` (`?role=watch`): up to 8 viewers (`MAX_WATCHERS`, 4003 "Too many viewers"), never counted as players; a viewer's messages are dropped, players' messages reach the other player and every viewer; viewers get `{"t":"peer","role","here"}` per player, players get `{"t":"watcher"}` and resend hello + shared. Pure rules in worker/room/src/rules.js (`admit`, `routeTargets`, `joinNotices`, `leaveNotices`), reused by js/room-fake.js. `hello` and `shared` carry the sender's `role`.
- Watching needs no `rooms` switch and is remembered in sessionStorage `pwc.watch` only (never `pwc.room`, never touches `pwc.state`). A guest refused with 4001 becomes a watcher. Display mode = `stage[data-display=on]` with `#display` (built in js/room-ui.js, pure view in js/room-display.js): read only, side by side (stacked under 700 px), nothing rotated, ignores the landscape setting, carries the watch QR. Players see a faint `#room-mark` line above the bar.
- Icons: all four (heart, log, fruit, flame) are flat, one fill, an outline in a darker shade of the same colour, light details.
